### **Description**
Take control of your academic life with Lumina, the smartest companion for students using Infinite Campus. Designed with a sleek, distraction-free interface, Lumina transforms your raw grades into powerful insights so you know exactly where you stand and where you need to focus.

Features:
• **Beautiful Trajectories:** See your grades visually. Our trajectory graphs show your exact percentage history so you can track your momentum throughout the semester.
• **AI Tutor:** Need to know what to study tonight? Upload your syllabus and ask Lumina’s AI Tutor. It analyzes your current grade and category weights to pinpoint exactly where you should focus to maximize your grade.
• **What-If Calculator:** See the future. Instantly drop low scores, add upcoming mock assignments, or simulate a final exam score to see exactly how it will impact your overall GPA.
• **Smart Dashboard:** Your GPA, days left in the semester, and a quick summary of your class stability, all beautifully organized on your home screen.
• **Missing Work Alerts:** Never let an assignment slip through the cracks. Lumina’s attention tab automatically flags missing assignments and unusually low scores from the current semester.

Lumina is built strictly for students. We never see your password, and your credentials stay securely inside your district’s portal.
