# Lumina: AI Academic Advisor

Lumina is a premium, AI-powered grade calculator and academic advisor built specifically for high school students. It seamlessly integrates with Infinite Campus to provide live grade tracking, advanced "what-if" calculations, and personalized AI tutoring based on actual course syllabi.

## Core Features

### 1. Live Infinite Campus Synchronization
- **One-Tap Sync:** Lumina instantly fetches all of your grades, assignments, and categories securely from Infinite Campus.
- **Custom Grading Scales:** Lumina honors your teacher's specific grading rules. If your teacher sets an 88% as an "A", Lumina displays it perfectly without overriding it.
- **Historical Tracking:** Automatically saves snapshots of your grades so you can see your academic trajectory over time.

### 2. The "What-If" Calculator
- **Dynamic Category Weights:** Calculates exactly how much a single assignment will impact your overall grade based on the teacher's exact category weights (e.g., 60% Tests, 40% Homework).
- **Interactive Toggles:** Drop or un-drop assignments to instantly see how your grade changes.
- **Add Fake Assignments:** Simulate upcoming tests or projects to see exactly what score you need to reach that next letter grade.

### 3. AI Syllabus Tutor
- **Multi-Format Upload:** Upload your course syllabus directly into the app (Supports PDF, Images, and Microsoft Word `.docx` via built-in extraction).
- **Context-Aware Advice:** Powered by Google's Gemini Vision AI, Lumina reads your syllabus and understands your teacher's late-work policies, extra credit rules, and grading rubrics.
- **Personalized Planning:** Ask the AI questions like "What is the late penalty for my homework?" and get instant answers specific to your current class.

### 4. Secure & Private Architecture
- **ClassLink SSO Integration:** Uses native WebViews to allow you to log in securely through your district's ClassLink or Azure SSO portal.
- **Zero-Storage Promise:** Your Infinite Campus login credentials are **never** seen, saved, or transmitted to any external server. All syncing happens directly on your device.
- **Multi-District Support:** Natively supports custom infinite campus domains and allows students to dynamically search and switch between different school districts.

### 5. Premium UI/UX Design
- **Sleek Aesthetics:** Features a beautiful dark mode, glassmorphism UI panels, and dynamic typography for a truly modern iOS experience.
- **Helpful Tooltips:** Built-in guidance markers easily explain advanced features to new users without cluttering the screen.
- **Performance Optimized:** Built on React and Capacitor for native-feeling performance and fluid animations.
